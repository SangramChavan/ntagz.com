Our story

Built around practical technology.

Ntagz brings accessible NFC and RFID hardware to businesses that want technology to work in the real world. Our focus is on useful products, straightforward ordering, customization options and dependable support, from small quantities to larger business requirements.

Ntagz is a brand of Ambivert Technologies Private Limited, a company based in Pune. Ambivert Technologies runs the Ntagz business, including orders, GST invoicing and customer support.

A note on the name: Ntagz was formerly known as NFCSnap.

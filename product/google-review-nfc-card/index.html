Frequently Asked Questions

Is the card already programmed when it arrives?

Yes. Send us your Google Business Profile review link (or just your listing name) when you order, and every card ships pre-programmed and ready — place it on the counter and it works immediately.

Can I change the link later, or reuse a card?

Yes — the NTAG215 chip is fully rewritable. Reprogram it anytime with any NFC-enabled phone using our free NFC Programmer tool, no special hardware needed.

Does it work on iPhone as well as Android?

Yes. iPhone 7 and newer read NFC tags natively through the Camera/Wallet layer — no app install needed on either platform.

What if a customer's phone doesn't support NFC?

It's rare on phones from the last several years, but for full coverage ask us about adding a printed QR code alongside the NFC chip as a fallback — send us a WhatsApp message and we'll set it up.

About the Google Review NFC Stand

A printed NFC counter stand carrying an NTAG215 chip, pre-programmed with your Google Business Profile review link before it ships. Tap it against any NFC-enabled Android phone or iPhone (7 and newer) and the customer's Google review form opens instantly — no app to download, no searching for your business. It comes in two versions: a compact 10×10 cm stand that's NFC-only, and a larger 12×12 cm stand with a printed QR code alongside the tap zone, so customers on phones without NFC can scan instead. Every stand is set up by us before dispatch, so it works the moment it reaches your counter.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the stand already programmed when it arrives?

Yes. Send us your Google Business Profile review link (or just your listing name) when you order. The NFC chip ships pre-programmed on both sizes, and the 12×12 cm stand's QR code is pre-printed with the same link — place it on the counter and it works immediately.

What's the difference between the two sizes?

The 10×10 cm stand is NFC-only — compact, for tight counters. The 12×12 cm stand has a larger panel with room for a printed QR code alongside the NFC tap zone, so customers without an NFC-capable phone can scan instead. The QR fallback isn't available on the smaller size — there isn't room on the panel.

Can I change the link later, or reuse a stand?

The NTAG215 chip is fully rewritable anytime with any NFC-enabled phone using our free NFC Programmer tool. The printed QR code on the 12×12 cm stand is fixed at print time — if you change your review link later, the NFC tap will follow it but the QR code won't.

Does it work on iPhone as well as Android?

Yes. iPhone 7 and newer read NFC tags natively — no app install needed on either platform. On the 10×10 cm NFC-only stand, a phone that can't read NFC has no fallback; the 12×12 cm stand's QR code covers that case.

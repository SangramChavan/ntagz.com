About the Google Review NFC Stand

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the stand already programmed when it arrives?

Yes. Send us your Google Business Profile review link (or just your listing name) when you order, and every stand ships pre-programmed and ready — place it on the counter and it works immediately.

What sizes does it come in?

12×12 cm and 10×10 cm. Both sit flat on a counter or checkout desk; pick whichever fits your space.

Can I change the link later, or reuse a stand?

Yes — the NTAG215 chip is fully rewritable. Reprogram it anytime with any NFC-enabled phone using our free NFC Programmer tool, no special hardware needed.

What if a customer's phone doesn't support NFC?

This NFC-only stand has no fallback for non-NFC phones. If you want full coverage, order the Google Review NFC + QR Stand instead — same design, with a printed QR code as a backup.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between NTAG215 and NTAG216?

NTAG216 has 888 bytes of usable memory versus 504 bytes on NTAG215 — useful if you need to store more than one link or a longer data record.

Can this be printed with a photo?

Yes, the blank white PVC surface works with sublimation printers, inkjet PVC printers, and third-party card printing services.

Is it compatible with all phones?

Any NFC-enabled Android phone and iPhone 7 or newer can read it without an app.

Do you offer double-sided printing?

Card printing itself isn't done in-house — we supply the blank NFC card; printing can be done locally or via our custom printing add-on (ask on WhatsApp).

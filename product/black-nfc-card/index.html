About the Black NFC 215 Card

Our black NFC business card carries an NTAG215 chip embedded inside standard CR80 credit-card-format PVC. Tap it against any NFC-enabled Android or iPhone (iPhone 7 and newer) and it instantly opens whatever you've programmed — a vCard contact save, your LinkedIn, a Google Review link, a UPI payment page, or a portfolio site. The matte-black finish is popular for UV/DTF custom printing since logos and white text print cleanly on it.

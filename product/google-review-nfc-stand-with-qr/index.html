About the Google Review NFC + QR Stand

Everything the NFC-only stand does, plus a printed QR code on the same panel carrying the same review link. Tap it against any NFC-enabled Android phone or iPhone (7 and newer) and the review form opens instantly with no scanning needed — but if a customer's phone doesn't support NFC, or they're more used to scanning codes, the QR code takes them to the exact same place. It's the safer default if you want to catch every customer, not just the ones with NFC-ready phones. Available in 12×12 cm and 10×10 cm.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the stand already programmed when it arrives?

Yes. Send us your Google Business Profile review link (or just your listing name) when you order, and every stand ships with both the NFC chip pre-programmed and the QR code pre-printed and ready — place it on the counter and it works immediately.

How is this different from the NFC-only stand?

Same size options and the same NFC tap experience, plus a printed QR code carrying the same review link. A customer whose phone doesn't support NFC — or who doesn't know to tap — can just scan the code instead. See the NFC-only stand if you don't need the QR fallback.

What sizes does it come in?

12×12 cm and 10×10 cm. Both sit flat on a counter or checkout desk; pick whichever fits your space.

Can I change the link later, or reuse a stand?

Yes — the NTAG215 chip is fully rewritable via our free NFC Programmer tool. The printed QR code stays fixed to whatever link it was printed with, so if you change your review link later the NFC tap will follow it but the QR code won't — reorder the panel if you need the QR updated too.

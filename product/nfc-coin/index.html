About the NFC Coin (25mm)

The coin format is thicker and more rigid than a flat adhesive sticker, which makes it better suited to rough handling, outdoor exposure, and being embedded into 3D objects, signage, or product bases rather than stuck flat on a surface. It uses the same widely-compatible NTAG215 chip as our business cards, so programming and read behaviour are identical.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this waterproof?

The PVC coin body is water-resistant for general outdoor exposure; for continuous submersion, ask us about our ruggedised/sealed variants.

Can I glue this into another object?

Yes, the coin format is designed to be embedded or glued into 3D-printed parts, wood, or plastic housings.

How is this different from a flat NFC sticker?

The coin is thicker and rigid, giving it better mechanical durability for outdoor or embedded use versus a thin adhesive sticker.

What's the read range?

Typically 2–4cm from a smartphone, same as our standard NTAG215 cards.

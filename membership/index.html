Questions

Good to know.

What is ntagz Membership?

A one-year membership that gives you member pricing on eligible ntagz products when you are signed in at checkout.

How much does it cost?

₹999 for 12 months, including GST. It is shown before you pay.

How do member prices work?

Member prices are a percentage off the regular price: currently a discount on NFC cards, tags and accessories and a discount on Google Review products and printed cards. Our server applies them at checkout. Quantity discounts apply as well.

Which products qualify for discounts?

The products in the calculator above. Items not listed there, such as the Sample Kit, are not discounted. If a product is not eligible, it is priced as normal.

How do I calculate my savings?

Net savings = regular product total − member product total − the membership fee. The calculator does this for any basket you build.

When does my membership start?

Immediately after your payment is verified, usually within seconds of paying.

How long is my membership valid?

12 months (365 days) from the purchase date.

Does membership renew automatically?

No. It is a one-time payment. When it ends you can choose to buy again.

Can I buy products without membership?

Yes. Membership is optional. Everything is available at regular prices.

What happens when my membership expires?

Your account stays, and orders switch back to regular prices. Past orders are not affected.

What is the cancellation and refund policy?

Membership-specific cancellation and refund terms are not published yet and need to be confirmed. Please email hello@ntagz.com before paying if this matters to you. Product orders follow the Returns & Refunds Policy.

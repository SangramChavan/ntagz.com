Be honest with yourself

Is Membership Worth It for You?

Membership pays off with volume. Whether it pays off for you depends on what you buy and how often.

Likely worth it

Frequent buyers, businesses ordering in bulk, agencies and resellers. The more eligible products you buy over the year, the more the member price adds up against the ₹999 fee.

Probably not worth it

One-time or small orders. A single small purchase rarely saves enough to cover the fee. Use the calculator above with your real basket to check.

Questions

Good to know.

How is qualifying spend calculated?

It is the value of your paid orders after any discounts, without GST and shipping. Only orders placed while you were signed in count. Pending, unpaid, cancelled and returned orders do not count.

When does my membership tier update?

As soon as an order is paid. Online payments update within moments of payment. UPI or bank-transfer orders count once we mark the payment received.

What happens after a refund?

A refund reduces the order’s qualifying value in proportion to the amount refunded, and a cancelled or returned order stops counting. If your spend drops below a threshold your tier can go down. Prices on past orders never change.

Do membership discounts apply to every product?

No. Some products are excluded (for example fixed-price kits), and some have a lower ceiling. We show a member price only where one applies, and there is a combined-discount limit that protects our margins.

Can coupons and member discounts be combined?

We do not use coupon codes. Cart milestone rewards may be running: a free-shipping reward can sit alongside your member price, but a percentage reward does not stack on member or quantity discounts unless its description says so. Your cart tells you which reward applies and why.

Does the membership expire?

Tiers are based on lifetime spend and do not expire. The optional paid Annual Membership lasts 12 months and does not renew automatically.

How do I access my membership on another device?

Sign in with the same email on any device. Your tier is stored on your account, not in the browser. We never show rewards based on an email or phone number alone.

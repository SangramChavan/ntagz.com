How the business works

Three steps from joining to earning.

  1. 01

    Join Ntagz

    Choose your partner plan and complete the online application.

  2. 02

    Start selling

    Buy products at partner pricing to resell, or register qualified customer leads for Ntagz to sell to.

  3. 03

    Earn from sales

    Earn resale margin, referral commission or service revenue, depending on your plan.

Product resale

You own the stock. You buy from Ntagz at your partner price, sell at your own price, and keep the difference. You deliver to and invoice your customer.

Referral selling

You hold no stock for the sale. You register the customer as your lead; Ntagz quotes, takes payment, ships and invoices. You earn a commission once the customer's payment clears and the refund window passes.

FAQ

Questions partners ask.

Who can become an Ntagz partner?

Individuals and registered businesses in India: retailers and shop owners, digital marketing agencies, freelancers and sales professionals, printing and electronics businesses, distributors and corporate resellers. You need a PAN and a proof of address to apply.

What is the minimum investment?

It depends on your plan. Every plan starts with an opening stock order, and the amount for each plan is shown in the plan cards on this page and in the first step of the application. The opening stock order comes back to you as products. Ntagz360 partners who take credit or a territory may also need a refundable security deposit.

Is there a joining fee?

No. You place an opening stock order instead, and its full value comes back to you as Ntagz products at your partner price. GST applies as usual.

How does the partner earn?

From genuine customer sales only: margin on stock you buy at partner price and resell, referral commission on orders from your own leads on plans that include it, and setup fees you charge your own customers on the Smile+ plan. Nobody earns anything for recruiting partners.

What is the difference between a distributor and a reseller?

A distributor (Ntagz360) covers a city or region, holds larger stock and serves many business customers there, with credit and a territory possible by agreement. A reseller (Enterprise, Smile+ or Smile) buys stock to sell to its own customers.

Can I operate from home?

Yes. Individuals can apply, and the Flex plan needs no shop because Ntagz quotes, ships and invoices your customers.

Can I become a partner without holding inventory?

Yes, on the Flex plan. You register your own leads and earn referral commission when they buy from Ntagz. Your small opening order becomes your demo kit.

Does Ntagz provide customer leads?

No. Ntagz does not promise leads. You find your own customers and register them in the partner portal, where each lead is protected so no other partner can claim the same contact.

Who handles shipping?

Ntagz ships your stock orders to you and ships the orders it sells to your referred customers. When you resell stock you hold, you deliver to your own customers.

Who generates customer invoices?

Ntagz issues a GST invoice for every order it sells, including your stock orders and orders from your referred customers. When you resell stock, you invoice your own customer.

Can I sell across multiple cities?

Yes. You can sell anywhere in India. Territories exist only for Ntagz360 partners by written agreement.

How does territory protection work?

Only on the Ntagz360 plan, and only by written agreement. A territory is tied to a yearly purchase target and is never sold on its own.

When are commissions paid?

After Ntagz approves the commission, the customer's payment has cleared and the refund window has passed. Ntagz then pays you by bank transfer or UPI and records the reference. TDS is deducted where it applies. Every commission and payout is visible in your partner dashboard.

Can I sell under my own brand?

White-label products are available to Ntagz360 partners by agreement. Custom printing with your customer's brand is available on selected products for every partner.

What happens if a customer requests a refund?

For orders Ntagz sold, the Ntagz refund policy applies and your commission is reduced or reversed to match. A refund after your commission was paid is reviewed with you. For stock you resold, your own terms with your customer apply, and product faults are handled under your partner agreement.

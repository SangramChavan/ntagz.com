How you earn

Two ways to earn. Both come from real sales.

Your plan decides which of these you use. Some plans use both.

1. Buy stock, resell it

You buy Ntagz products at your partner price, which is below the price on ntagz.com. You sell them to your customers at your own price and keep the difference.

  1. Buy at partner price
  2. Sell to your customer
  3. Keep the margin

2. Refer a customer, Ntagz sells

You register a customer as your lead in the partner portal. Ntagz quotes, takes payment and ships. You earn a commission on that order.

  1. Register your lead
  2. Customer pays Ntagz
  3. Commission after the refund window

Commission is paid only on your own leads, after the customer's payment clears and the refund window passes. A refund reduces it. Your partner price and commission terms are shared in writing when your application is approved.

Questions

Straight answers.

Is there a joining fee?

No. You place an opening stock order instead, and every rupee of it comes back to you as Ntagz products at your partner price. The size of the order depends on your plan and is confirmed when your application is approved. GST applies as usual.

How much can I earn?

That depends entirely on what you sell. Ntagz does not promise or guarantee any income. You earn margin on stock you resell and, on plans that include it, commission on orders from your own leads.

Do I earn for bringing in other partners?

No. Ntagz never pays anyone for recruiting partners. You earn only from products sold to customers.

When is referral commission paid?

After Ntagz approves it, the customer's payment has cleared and the refund window has passed. If the customer gets a refund, the commission goes down to match. You can see every commission, its status and any change in your partner dashboard, and raise a question on any of them.

Can I get a territory or credit?

On the Ntagz360 plan, yes, by agreement. A territory comes with a yearly purchase target, and credit may need a refundable security deposit.

What do I need to apply?

Your business details, your PAN and a proof of address. Documents are stored privately and are seen only by the Ntagz team reviewing your application.

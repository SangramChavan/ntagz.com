How they fit together

Ntagz makes the product. TapInHere is the destination.

A physical product on one side, a digital profile on the other, and a tap or scan that joins them.

  1. 01

    Physical product

    An NFC card, coin or tag, or a printed QR code, that people can tap or scan.

    Supplied by Ntagz
  2. 02

    Digital destination

    Your TapInHere profile: who you are, what you offer and how to reach you, on one page you can edit any time.

    Supplied by TapInHere
  3. 03

    Customer interaction

    Someone taps an NFC phone or scans with a camera. Your profile opens in the browser, with no app to install.

    Tap or scan

Good to know: a product only opens your profile once your TapInHere link has been written to the tag or printed as a QR code. Products are not linked to a TapInHere account automatically, and Ntagz does not create your account.

How the setup works depends on the product

  • Blank NFC cards and tags: write your TapInHere profile link yourself with the free Ntagz NFC Programmer. Blank products are rewritable, so you can change the link later. The Programmer needs Chrome on an Android phone (Web NFC).
  • Custom printed cards: the custom printing option includes printing and programming. Share your artwork and your profile link when you order.
  • QR codes: TapInHere generates your profile’s QR code for free, on your device. Print it yourself or ask us about a custom QR product.
  • Google review stands: these ship pre-programmed with your Google review link, not a TapInHere profile.

First create your TapInHere link. It is free to start; paid plans add reviews, analytics and teams (see TapInHere pricing).
